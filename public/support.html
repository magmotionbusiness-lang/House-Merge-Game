How do I play?

Drag to aim and let go to slide your building up the blueprint. Two identical buildings that touch merge into the next, bigger one. Keep the pile behind the red line. The glowing ring shows when your shot will merge; a rainbow ring means a big chain.

I bought Remove Ads on another phone

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app (or "Offload App" with data removal) erases it. There is no cloud save yet.

Ads

Ads between rounds never appear in your first rounds, at most once every two minutes, and never mid-game. Rewarded ads are always your choice. Remove Ads turns off the between-round ads for good. To change ad personalisation, see Settings → Privacy & ads in the game, or read the privacy policy.

No sound

Check that the silent switch on the side of your iPhone is off, and the volumes in Settings are up.
