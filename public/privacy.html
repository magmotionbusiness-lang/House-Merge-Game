Privacy Policy

Blueprint Merge · Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Blueprint Merge has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress (scores, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which has its own privacy rules, described below.

What stays on your device

Game progress, settings and your Remove Ads purchase status are saved locally in the app. Deleting the app deletes this data. We never receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device model and ad interactions in order to show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud.

  • Tracking permission: on iPhone, the app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", AdMob does not use the advertising identifier and shows non-personalised ads. You see the same number of ads either way.
  • EU, UK and other regions with consent laws: Google's consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only which items you own.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Change ad tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change ad consent: in the game, Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Remove all game data: delete the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page. Questions: see the support page.
